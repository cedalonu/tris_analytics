# Phase 63 — Temporal rank stability
Ranks candidates at chronological cutoffs using only snapshots recorded at or before each cutoff. The date sequence is divided into up to the requested number of periods (default 3), reporting rank range, top-half frequency, period coverage, and a descriptive stability score.
This measures temporal consistency of research triage only. It is not prediction, probability, or evidence of lottery advantage. Cutoffs are not optimized against draw outcomes; candidates with sparse history may appear in fewer periods, and no missing history is imputed.
