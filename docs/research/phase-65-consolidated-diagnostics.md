# Phase 65 — Consolidated diagnostics and dependency map
## Dependencies
Imported snapshots → history quality → eligibility gate → priority score/queue. Robustness grid measures threshold sensitivity. Rank-stability grid measures config sensitivity. Chronological snapshots feed temporal stability and degradation/recovery. Consolidated diagnostics combine findings into executive status.

## Categories
- OK: adequate history and no current warnings.
- REVISAR: gate review, shallow history, fragile thresholds, low rank/temporal stability.
- PROBLEMA: structural defects, blocked gate, or degraded state.
- INSUFICIENTE: fewer than two snapshots.

## Limits
Operational workflow/data diagnostic only. Scores are descriptors, not calibrated probabilities, predictive accuracy or evidence of lottery advantage. Do not treat as betting signals.
