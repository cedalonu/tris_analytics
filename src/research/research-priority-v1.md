# Phase 58 — Research priority matrix
Ranks the research-review workload only. It is not a predictor, number ranking, probability, or evidence of lottery advantage.
Components (0–100): history quality 40%; depth (linear to 8 revisions) 20%; adjacent-class stability 25%; fixed current-class descriptive coding 15% (favorable/unfavorable observed 70, neutral 45, contradictory 15, insufficient/other 25).
Eligibility overrides score: blocked and review are outside the queue; eligible hypotheses receive high (>=75), medium (>=55), or low priority.
